How We Think

Principle 01

Operators, Not Tourists

We’ve sat in the seats. We’ve managed the P&L. We understand that a 2% improvement in a core workflow is worth more than a 50% improvement in a task that doesn’t move the needle.

Principle 02

System Rigidity

Complexity is the enemy of execution. We build systems that are rigid in their logic but flexible in their scale. If a system requires a human to "check" it daily, it's not a system—it's a chore.
