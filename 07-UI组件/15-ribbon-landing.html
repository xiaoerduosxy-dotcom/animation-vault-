One source of truth for every pixel you ship.

Stop reconciling tokens across tools. Ribbon unifies your design language into a living system that designers, engineers, and PMs all edit together.

01 / Tokens

Semantic tokens, versioned.

Primitive to semantic to component tokens — with full diff history, branching, and a review flow your engineers will actually use.

02 / Components

Components that stay in sync.

Bi-directional sync between Figma and your codebase. Props, variants, and states are authored once and flow everywhere.

03 / Themes

Themes without the tangle.

Dark mode, brand skins, density modes, accessibility variants. Compose them with overrides — not copies.

04 / Docs

Docs that write themselves.

Every component page is generated from the source. Usage guidelines live next to the thing they describe.

05 / Pipelines

Ship via your own CI.

Publish tokens as npm, CSS, Swift, Kotlin, or anything custom. Hook into Git, Linear, and your design review process.

06 / Governance

Review, approve, enforce.

Role-based access, contribution guidelines, and automatic lint rules keep the system consistent as your team scales.
