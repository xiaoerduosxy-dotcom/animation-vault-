Cataloging the mechanics of digital physics and pure interface motion.

Kinematics Algorithmic WebGL CSS.Render

This archive serves as a repository for experimental motion studies. By stripping away narrative context and utilitarian interface elements, we isolate the pure mechanics of easing, velocity, and spatial transformation.

Our methodology treats code as a physical material. The exhibits below demonstrate systemic behaviors—from simulated fluid dynamics to rigid body interactions—rendered natively within the browser environment.
